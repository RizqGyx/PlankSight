PlankSight

PlankSight is an iOS fitness app that helps users maintain proper plank form through real-time pose detection and instant corrective feedback, using a fully on-device camera-based body tracking system.

🎯 Background / Problem Statement

Most people who plank alone have no idea if their form is actually correct — and regular fitness apps don't help either, because their timer keeps running even when your posture is wrong. So the time you record doesn't reflect how well you actually trained. PlankSight fixes this by watching your body through the camera and only counting time when your form is right. The moment something breaks — like your hips drooping or your knees bending — the timer pauses and your phone tells you what to fix.

✨ Key Features
  • Tracks your body joints live via camera — no internet or extra hardware needed
  • Timer only counts when your form is actually correct — bad reps don't count
  • Session starts and stops automatically — just get into plank position, no buttons
  • Speaks out loud when posture breaks — tells you exactly what's wrong in real time
  • Tracks your daily streak and unlocks 19 achievement badges as you improve
  • Home screen widget — shows your streak without even opening the app

🛠️ Tech Stack

SwiftUI, AVFoundation
Apple Vision, Swift Charts
WidgetKit, Combine
